## Why

A static portfolio has no excuse to be slow or to track people. Speed is also something a
visitor notices before reading a word.

## Looks like

- Mostly static HTML with a small JavaScript budget.
- Self-hosted fonts, no trackers and no third-party requests.
- Lighthouse scores held in CI.

## Not this

- Analytics, embeds or CDNs added "just because".
