## Why

An engineer's portfolio is judged on the basics. Accessibility, responsive layout and a
theme that respects the visitor's setting are part of the work being shown, not extras.

## Looks like

- WCAG AA contrast in light and dark, keyboard access everywhere, a visible focus ring.
- The visitor's theme and reduced-motion settings are respected.
- Everything readable with JavaScript off.

## Not this

- Accessibility checked once by hand and then forgotten. It's enforced in CI.
