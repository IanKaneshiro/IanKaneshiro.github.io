## Why

Most visitors are recruiters, hiring managers or engineers who arrive from a link and give
the page a few seconds. If those seconds don't answer "who, doing what, how good, how do I
reach them", the site has failed at its main job.

## Looks like

- Name, role, current focus and contact links all on the first screen, on a phone too.
- Projects and stack are scannable without reading paragraphs.
- Reaching out is one click.

## Not this

- A long life story above the fold.
- A contact form (there's no backend, and a mailto link is enough).
